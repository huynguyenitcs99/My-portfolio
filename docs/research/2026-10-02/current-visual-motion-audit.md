# Current portfolio — visual and motion audit

Inspected 2026-10-02. Source: fixed `docs/design/storyboard-v2.png`, latest existing desktop/mobile captures, the source components/styles, and live production Chromium at localhost:3000 with 1440×900 and 390×844 viewports. Live screenshots: `/tmp/huy-audit/{1440,390}-{identity,work,creative-method,daily-smith,slide-design,engineering,playground,about}.png`. This review is about whether the design communicates the requested personal brand; it does not dispute previous functional checks.

## Main conclusion

The implementation now resembles the eight static storyboard compositions, but the compositions themselves and their motion contract underserve the original objective: a memorable AI engineer and creative builder, with visual transformations that explain the work and make personal contributions credible. Passing overflow, keyboard and Lighthouse checks did not establish that brand/story quality. The latest user instruction authorizes revisiting the underlying design.

## Concrete findings

1. **The sequence behaves as a long illustrated article, not a connected transformation.** `Arrival` rotates the whole card group 0–12° and translates it 0–70px as the section leaves. `OrbitShowcase` independently rotates a single menu PNG -9–0° and scales .86–1. There is no card moving from the orbit into the next hero. The apparent hero/work connection in the storyboard remains unimplemented.
2. **Daily Smith does not animate causality.** The cube, glowing lines, lights and foliage are baked into `daily.png`. Sources and outputs enter with a generic 18px reveal. Nothing visibly travels from a source, becomes context, then resolves into a priority. The labels describe architecture but provide no concrete illustrative daily-use scenario.
3. **Slide Design does not separate editable layers.** `slide.png` already contains the exploded planes. The scroll variable moves and rotates the whole raster. Viewers never see an image become separately addressable text/image/shape layers, which is the most marketable demonstration of this capability.
4. **Remotion is present but adds little explanatory value.** `BrandStudy` overlays a linear moving playhead and time counter over a generated editing-timeline JPEG. This indicates a library is installed but is not evidence of a creative workflow or system. It should instead author a specific before/during/after transformation with independently composed elements.
5. **Art overwhelms the strongest evidence.** Live computed sizes: Creative role 12px desktop /10px mobile; the actual personal-contribution paragraph 12px/11px; concept notes 10px/8px; Daily role 16px/11px. These are the facts that should earn trust, yet giant headings and decorative scenes get the emphasis. Meaningful paragraph text should be 15–18px on mobile/desktop, supporting labels around12–14px.
6. **Mobile pacing is especially long.** At390×844: hero710px, Creative775px, visual DNA1282px, Daily969px, Slide708px, engineering1231px, playground699px, contact829px. Daily begins at document y2842, Slide3826, contact6465. The hero, then menu art, then a tall schema delay learning the breadth of current work. A concise capabilities/ownership strip should arrive within the first 1–2 screens.
7. **The method graphic weakens the distinction it should explain.** Its four reference tiles are crops of the same generated menu collage. The displayed JSON is independent style/tone/elements/typography keys, visually resembling the independent design-axis assembly Huy moved beyond. It does not communicate relationships or preservation of a coherent reference system. Keep intact reference layouts and show hierarchy, composition and relationships carried into a guideline and across formats; present the actual adoption by the original poster owner prominently.
8. **Generated decoration is too dominant for a trust-led engineering portfolio.** The homepage has a generated portrait, menu output, cube, slide, engineering covers and exploration grid. Disclaimers are honest, but real engineering media are hidden inside links. Bring at least one verified artifact and one specific ownership statement per current/legacy case onto the homepage. Use one well-placed provenance caption per scene rather than multiple competing disclaimers.
9. **The palette is coherent but the visual grammar is mostly botanical/editorial.** Paper leaves, food collage, tan brush edges and serif headlines read as lifestyle/art-direction branding more readily than inventive engineering. Earth/plum may stay, but use a clearer light/dark contrast rhythm, precision in diagrams, purposeful technical details and a signature twin-tail motif. The exact chosen logo need not change.
10. **Identity is partly obscured and hierarchy competes.** The foreground portrait covers the surname on desktop and mobile. Three enlarged rectangular cards, handwritten notes, ellipse paths and paper tears compete simultaneously. Reserve one primary focal object, put the complete readable name in a protected area, and make the remaining elements support it.
11. **The gallery is visually broad but conceptually thin.** Uncaptioned mountains/architecture/leaves show aesthetic taste, not what Huy explored or built. Replace anonymous tiles with 3 titled studies and one-line intent/tool/result context. The Remotion study should be worth watching by itself.
12. **Public terminology should be visitor-friendly.** Replace “PIC” in visible copy with specific ownership such as “Led the AI pipeline” or “Lead engineer”; preserve contributor distinctions for Creative Studio. Released company features and personal concept studies need different small clear status labels.

## Recommended scroll choreography

Use one signature sequence near the top and two shorter explanatory sequences. Native scroll controls progress; never require an initial click or waiting through an intro. Copy and final states remain readable when JavaScript is absent, when scrolling quickly and under reduced motion.

- **Arrival / orientation:** immediately readable name, AI engineer × creative builder, one clear positioning sentence and three concise current-work outcomes. A 0.7–1.0s once-only orbit assembly may introduce depth. No perpetual card carousel. Nekomata/twin curves are a recognizable personal signature.
- **Orbit → focused project:** over about120–160vh on desktop, cardA moves on its own to center, grows and straightens; portrait/name recede gently; the same card plane becomes the project stage. The two remaining projects stay visible as small labeled anchors until the next scene settles. Hold the fully readable result for a meaningful portion of progress. This directly answers the Ex-Aid selection inspiration through abstract choreography, without copying franchise imagery.
- **Creative DNA / insight:** show one intact reference set; a deliberate scan reveals a small connected grammar card; a few design traits travel together into a menu composition; the same coherent grammar fans out into supported format labels. Pair it with “I developed the visual-DNA method for menus; the original poster-pipeline owner later adopted it across other formats.” Concept visual only until real approved outputs exist. Do not manufacture benchmark comparisons.
- **Daily / utility:** two synthetic, explicitly illustrative email/calendar items travel into a clean context boundary; dependencies resolve; one concrete priority and a follow-up prompt form on the right. Keep source provenance connected to the brief to explain trust. Only supported read/context/conversation capabilities.
- **Slide / transformation:** a genuine HTML/SVG composition begins as a single framed image, then its text/image/shape layers separate into editable DOM/SVG planes, then settle back into a readable slide. Keep “In development” in the fixed scene header. This is a capability illustration, not production output evidence.
- **Engineering proof / pause:** reduce motion and show actual available legacy media with individual role and concrete scoped outcome. This is the credibility beat after spectacle.
- **Playground / range:** a tight 3-study strip with authored short loops and a static poster for each, title and purpose; pause offscreen. Distinguish personal experiments from company work.
- **Contact / resolve:** return twin-tail curves to the logo and move to calm, readable copy and direct contact. One clear action, supporting LinkedIn/GitHub links.

## Mobile behavior

Keep hero under ~1 viewport where practical, without tiny role text. Use a single-column composition with outcomes visible in normal flow. Convert the long orbit takeover into a short200–350px transition or simply a shared card entering normal flow; avoid a multi-screen pin. DNA can be three compact steps with a sticky illustration only if it genuinely reduces reading effort. Daily should show the whole input/context/output chain in roughly one readable screen, followed by prose. Engineering should use compact rows with visible preview and role/outcome, not three huge stacked cards. No mandatory horizontal swipe. Reduced motion displays complete readable states, not hidden fragments. Keyboard focus, native anchors, offscreen pause and no-JS rendering already work and should be retained.

## Asset / Remotion requirements

Generate isolated visual layers, not one screenshot-like flattened webpage: transparent brand object/texture, reference artwork, individually prepared menu outputs, background plates. Author all meaningful text in HTML/SVG. Use Remotion for deterministic short sequences where frame control helps (DNA transformation or slide reconstruction); bind frame selection to scroll or render a compressed loop with a poster as appropriate. Site navigation, typography and scroll continuity belong in DOM/CSS/Motion. A moving playhead is not sufficient evidence of a rich Remotion scene. Keep image provenance and delivery formats explicit.
