Chat Smith got a huge makeover. Update the app to see what’s new, switch up your theme, and give GPT-6 Sol and GPT-Image 2.5 a try while you’re there.

Meet Chat Smith, your personal AI chatbot and assistant for work, study, and everyday life. Built on advanced AI models including GPT-6 Sol, GPT-5.6, GPT-5, GPT-4, GPT-4o, Claude Sonnet 5, Claude Haiku 4.5, Gemini 3, DeepSeek V4, and Grok-4.5, Chat Smith helps you write, create, research, plan, and get more done.

Whether you need an AI writer, coding assistant, or deep research tool, Chat Smith brings your productivity tools together in one app.

WHAT’S NEW IN CHAT SMITH?

• AI Humanizer: Make AI-generated text sound natural and human. Rewrite AI content in a style that feels more like you.
• AI Detector: Detect AI-generated and AI-assisted content. Check your writing before submitting essays, articles, or important work.
• AI Logo Design: Create logos with an AI logo generator. Explore styles, customize designs, and bring your brand to life.
• Calendar Smith Agent: Use an AI calendar assistant to plan events, schedule meetings, and set reminders with a simple prompt.

KEY FEATURES

AI IMAGE GENERATOR
Create standout AI images in seconds for social media, business, and creative projects. Turn prompts or photos into high-quality visuals with advanced text-to-image and image-to-image technology. Powered by OpenAI GPT-Image 2.5, Gemini Nano Banana Pro, and advanced AI image generation.

AI WRITING ASSISTANT
Generate outlines or paragraphs with an AI essay writer, and craft stories with an AI story generator built on GPT-6, Gemini 3, Claude, DeepSeek, and Grok.

DEEP RESEARCH
Go deeper with Pro Search and Deep Research. Get accurate answers, trusted sources, and up-to-date reports powered by advanced AI reasoning.

VIRTUAL ASSISTANT
Plan tasks, organize ideas, and get things done faster with an AI assistant powered by GPT-6 Sol, GPT-5, Gemini 3, Claude, DeepSeek, and Grok.

MARKETING ASSISTANT
Generate ad copy, SEO keywords, product names, brand taglines, and social media content. Create posts and captions for Facebook, X, and Instagram in seconds.

REAL-TIME SEARCH
Powered by GPT-6 Sol, Claude, DeepSeek V3, Grok-4, and Gemini 3, this feature gives you fast access to the latest information online, from breaking news to niche queries.

EMAIL GENERATOR
Create polished emails in seconds for sales, announcements, promotions, and more.

AI HOMEWORK HELPER
Get homework help, study notes, quiz prep, and AI math support. Snap a photo for step-by-step guidance with AI Math Solver, powered by GPT-6, GPT-4o, and GPT-4.

TEXT & VIDEO SUMMARIZER
Summarize documents, PDFs, notes, and YouTube videos. Get key takeaways, rewrites, and translations in seconds.

AI TRANSLATOR
Translate text, messages, and documents with fast, natural multilingual AI translation.

AI KEYBOARD
Type faster with AI-powered grammar fixes, predictive typing, auto replies, and autocorrect.

GRAMMAR AND SPELLING CHECKER
Fix grammar, spelling, and punctuation with AI proofreading tools.

RESUME & CV BUILDER
Create resumes and cover letters, optimize LinkedIn profiles, and prepare interview questions with an AI resume checker.

MOBILE-WEB SYNC
Use Chat Smith on mobile and web at chatsmith.io with seamless cross-device syncing.
