## Material unresolved issues

1. **The logo is a redraw, not the exact mark.** The drafts retain two tails, but their head, body/feet and tail contours differ from [the source Nekomata](/workspace/My-portfolio/public/images/brand/nekomata.png). Place the approved source mark into the final draft rather than treating the generated approximation as preserved identity.

2. **Contribution copy needs one consistent, factual pass.** Desktop C's continuation labels the Creative role “Design + direction,” while the mobile Daily chapter has “PIC” followed by an empty line. The Creative storyboard explains DNA research better, but its adoption line leaves out the poster owner's role. Use the known contribution throughout: Daily — PIC for AI pipeline, data flow and agent layer; Creative — contributor who adapted posters to menus and researched coherent reference-image DNA/JSONL guidelines, subsequently adopted by the poster owner across formats; Slide — main PIC, unreleased. Separate released product status from illustrative preview status. Replace “reference image layout” with wording that communicates the coherent guideline method.

3. **Mobile's strongest trust cue is too small.** At a representative 390px display width, the Creative preview's subtitle and “My contribution: DNA guidelines” line scale to roughly 8–10px. The face, name and overlapping stream work, but the contribution cannot carry its intended weight at that size. Give the personal-contribution line readable emphasis; simplify the preview artwork or show one dominant preview with compact next-project cues. The first Daily chapter should also expose its complete role without the placeholder.

4. **The storyboard demonstrates endpoints, not the requested scroll morph.** The context-to-brief, reference-to-guideline and image-to-elements sequences are meaningful. The strip does not yet specify how the selected hero card remains the same object while expanding, what persists between scenes, or where reading pauses occur. Annotate one retained card/element and add a few intermediate states for a single transition before a motion proof. Until such a proof exists, describe this as an authored storyboard; smooth scroll, readable timing, responsive behavior and motion quality remain unverified.

No image-only draft establishes interaction, accessibility or performance. Those checks belong to a later implemented proof, not this visual review. The separately identified engineering-placeholder correction is excluded from these findings.
